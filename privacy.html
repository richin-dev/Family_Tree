Privacy Policy

Family Tree · Last updated October 29, 2026

This Family Tree website is managed for family members. It displays family history and lets authorized family members view information and photographs. The family administrator manages accounts and the information shown on the site.

Information on the family tree

The tree may contain names, family relationships, dates, notes, and photographs. It may also contain contact details or other sensitive family information entered by the administrator. Visitors who are not signed in may see basic family-tree information. Contact details and blood group information are withheld from public visitors. Photographs are available only to signed-in family members.

Photographs and Google Drive

When the family administrator connects Google Drive and uploads a photograph, the website uses Google Drive to store and retrieve that photograph. The website requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, which is used for files created by this app. The app does not make its photo files public. A signed-in family member can view a photo through the website; only the family administrator can upload or remove photos.

Google handles information sent to Drive under Google's own terms and privacy policy. You can review or revoke this app's Google access in your Google Account settings.

Where information is stored

The family tree and the references needed to connect people with photos are stored using Cloudflare's hosting and database services. Photo files uploaded through the app are stored in the connected Google Drive account. The app uses its hosting services to process sign-ins and provide access to the site.

Who can see information

Basic family-tree information may be visible to anyone who can access the website. Additional family information and photographs require a family sign-in. The family administrator controls who receives an account. We do not sell family information. Cloudflare and Google process information as needed to host the website and provide the Drive features described above.

Retention and requests

Tree information remains on the site until the family administrator changes or removes it. The administrator can remove a photo through the app; the corresponding photo file is then deleted from the connected Drive account. Google may retain deleted files in Drive Trash according to that account's settings. For questions, corrections, or removal requests, contact the family administrator using the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the website's features or data practices change. The current version will be available at this address.
